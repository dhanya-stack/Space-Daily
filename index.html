Why do we always see the same side of the Moon?

The Moon turns at the same rate it orbits Earth, keeping nearly the same face pointed our way.

Read the explanation +

The Moon rotates on its axis once in about the same time it takes to orbit Earth. This synchronisation is called tidal locking. The Moon has a far side, but it is not permanently dark.
