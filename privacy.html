SPACE DAILY

Privacy, simply put.

Space Daily does not offer accounts, and it does not run analytics or advertising trackers.

If you choose interests, this browser stores your selected topics using localStorage. You can clear them using the “Clear preferences” control on the website or by clearing site storage.

If you subscribe to the newsletter, your email address is submitted directly to Kit, which processes it to provide the newsletter service. Kit’s own privacy terms apply to that processing.

The site displays content hosted by NASA and may load fonts from Google Fonts. Those services may receive connection metadata according to their policies. If aggregate analytics are added later, they must collect no sensitive information, avoid cross-site tracking, and be disclosed here before use.
