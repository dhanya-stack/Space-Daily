Privacy, simply put.

This is a prototype. It does not offer live subscriptions or accounts, and it does not run analytics or advertising trackers.

If you choose interests, this browser stores your selected topics using localStorage. You can clear them using the “Clear preferences” control on the website or by clearing site storage.

The newsletter form does not submit your address to a server. It can only copy your preferred subscription details to your clipboard. Do not treat it as a live subscription.

The site displays content hosted by NASA and may load fonts from Google Fonts. Those external services may receive connection metadata according to their respective policies. A production version needs an updated privacy notice, appropriate controls, and consent/opt-out handling before deploying analytics and email subscriptions.
